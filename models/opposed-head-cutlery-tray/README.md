## Intended use

- Dry clean forks and spoons, stored horizontally in drawers or on desks. Reference head width 46 mm, handle width 14 mm, head length ≤66 mm, total length ≤230 mm; measure curves, protrusions and bowl height separately.
- Leave at least 2 mm above stacks; do not infer count from tray height or force items across the divider. No lid or lock, blade protection, spill-proof transport or seal. Check material and hygiene suitability for food contact.

## Printing and assembly

- Two sets of dry cutlery lie flat with heads and handles reversed; maximum head width 46 mm, straight handle width 14 mm, head length ≤ 66 mm, overall length ≤ 230 mm. Measure actual bent handles, bumps and spoon bowl height separately.
- Front and back each have a 49.0 mm wide head zone and a 32.0 mm narrow zone, with a folded shared wall in the middle; compartment floors are the same height; items go in and out from above and must not be forced diagonally along the boundary.
- Two finger openings sit outside the wide heads, each 28 mm clear; an 8 mm edge wall is kept below. Leave at least 2 mm above the measured stack height; do not infer how many cutlery sets fit from the tray height.
- One-piece, base down, with no assembly hardware, lid or latch. Only for storing dry, clean cutlery in horizontal drawers/on desks; not for blade cut protection, spill-proof transport or sealing.
- Preview colors are for reference only; it can be printed in a single color. Check slicing before printing; before use, check actual access, hygiene and the suitability of food contact materials; test-fit with your actual cutlery and check the printing material before use.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
