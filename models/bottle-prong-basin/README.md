## Printing and assembly

- One-piece, printed flat on its base. Empty bottles go upside down on the cross posts; the post outer diameter must be smaller than the bottle mouth clear diameter; the slots run from the post tops down to the water collection space at the bottom.
- The bottle's outer diameter should be smaller than the position center distance; measure the center of gravity and tip resistance; empty the water promptly. Before use, check food contact, leakage or drying efficiency.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
