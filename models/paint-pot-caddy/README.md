## Printing and assembly

- Print the basket and perforated plate flat base down separately; the perforated plate rests on the ledges on both sides.
- Once the bottles are empty, the perforated plate lifts off the locating holes; set hole diameter with allowance for the measured bottle body.
- Fixed 0.4 mm single-side fit; pairs only within this set; fit for specific brand bottle shapes or anti-tip performance is not promised.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
