## Printing and assembly

- Print flat base down; the wide base feet go under the books with the side pocket facing out. No wall fixing; check tipping resistance against your actual book weight.
- Units are millimeters. Measure clearance from your actual items first; check slicing before printing and test-fit the key mating parts.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
