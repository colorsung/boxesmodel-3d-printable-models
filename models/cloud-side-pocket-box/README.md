## Printing and assembly

- The central main compartment holds everyday items, with small end compartments for accessories; each side can stay one large cell or split into two.
- One-piece, printed flat on its base; the arc outline and straight base edge share one floor, with no moving joints or hidden hardware.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
