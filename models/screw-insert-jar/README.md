## Printing and assembly

- Seen from above, unscrew the whole lid counterclockwise; it rises one pitch per turn; lift only after it is fully off the thread. The lid's underside rests on the shoulder rather than being stopped by bottoming the threads.
- After unscrewing, grip the central lift post and lift the sorting tray straight out; the inner tray sits on the jar floor with a fixed 0.5 mm outer clearance and a solid bottom.
- Radial dividers per compartment; the lift post is 6 mm above the dividers; do not cover it with items. The compartments are not closed individually on top; keep it upright, and mixing when flipped is not prevented.
- Test-print to check the 0.35 mm nominal thread clearance first; no airtight, watertight, food contact or transport anti-loosening performance is promised. Slice with the jar/inner tray bottom down and the lid top down.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
