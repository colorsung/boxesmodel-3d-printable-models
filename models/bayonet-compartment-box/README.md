## Printing and assembly

- Line up with the three entrances under the lid, press down and turn about 45° clockwise (seen from above) to the stop; turn back counterclockwise to line up with the entrance, then lift.
- The preview shows the locked position; once locked it stops lifting straight out, but there is no elastic anti-back-out catch; transport loosening resistance or liquid sealing is not guaranteed.
- Compartment depth is measured from the top of the floor to the box opening; the dividers are slightly lower than the lid; each compartment loads and unloads from the top.
- Box bottom down; flip the lid so the closed top is on the plate; check bridging/supports at the top of the twist slots; test-print to check the fit clearance first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
