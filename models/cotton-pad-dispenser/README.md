## Printing and assembly

- Soft round cotton pads load from the top, and a lower side window lets you fold one lightly to take it; not for rigid discs of the same diameter.
- The round compartment diameter is a clear size; leave allowance for your actual cotton pads; the side compartment can hold cotton swabs and similar items. Base down; check bridging at the top edge of the side windows when slicing.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
