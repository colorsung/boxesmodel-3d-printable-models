# Tapered Fluted Planter with Saucer

A tapered planter drains into a separate raised saucer. Lift the pot to empty the saucer; continuous shallow grooves follow the tapered wall.

## Printing and assembly

- Both parts are included and need no purchased fittings. The pot rests on three saucer pads and lifts off vertically.
- Empty the saucer promptly after catching drips. It is not a validated water reservoir; watertightness and growing performance are untested.
- Slice the pot and saucer separately with bases down. Small growing-media particles may pass through the drains; choose the hole diameter to suit the medium.
- Preview colors are not included in STL/STEP.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
