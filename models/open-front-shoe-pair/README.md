# Open-Front Two-Shoe Rack

A pair of shoes rests on separate upper and lower supports, with no bar across the front opening. A front finger notch on the upper tray makes shoes easy to take; four posts locate the whole tray so it lifts off, and the vented lower rack can be wiped directly.

## Printing and assembly

- Complete printed parts: 1 full-floor four-shoulder vented lower rack and 1 upper tray/rear stop with locating blind holes, 2 in total; the four guide posts are printed in one piece, with no purchased assembly hardware or custom metal parts.
- For one pair of dry indoor shoes, stored upper and lower. With the rack empty and level, line up the four blind holes with the lower rack's guide posts and lower it straight onto the wide bottom shoulders; posts only locate; wide shoulders carry the load. Take both shoes off first, then lift the whole upper tray straight up to wipe the lower compartment. Do not move the whole rack by lifting only the upper tray; locating is not locking.
- Lower compartment conservative straight-through clear width 126, depth 233.8, height 107.6 mm; for real shoes, subtract the room needed to take them; fit is not inferred from shoe size or brand. No front bar; slide each shoe forward first; a front center finger notch on the upper tray makes the soles easy to reach. Lower-level shoes longer than the clear depth or taller than the clear height are not suitable.
- In front of the upper level's rear stop the usable depth is 233.8 mm, the clear width between the two rear side stops is 141.6 mm, and the front finger notch is 44 × 32 mm. Shoe soles must not rest only over the finger notch; the software reference envelope needs at least 60% of the sole's projected area supported. Hold the lower rack when taking a shoe so the other one does not fall.
- The page's outer size of 150 × 238 × 132 mm is the empty rack envelope, excluding shoes. Upper tray surface 116 mm above the desk; total clear height inside the cabinet must add the measured height of the upper whole shoe plus access allowance, and leave room in front to pull the whole shoe out.
- Four Ø6 × 2 mm posts, Ø6.7 × 2.35 mm blind holes; 0.35 mm radial and hole bottom clearance each, with 1.85 mm of solid material above the hole. All parts must be generated with the same parameters and not scaled individually.
- Print the lower rack base down; choose orientation and supports for the upper tray according to the stop lip/blind hole direction; confirm the side window sloped top, blind holes and upper tray warping when slicing. Check large parts against your printer's usable build volume; not every size is promised to fit a 256 mm bed.
- Slicing, test prints, real fit, load-bearing, tip resistance and long-term creep are verified separately. Not a stool, step, wet shoe drip pan or portable closed shoe box; no load rating is given.
- Colors: cream frame/lake blue rest, off-white frame/sage rest, warm gray frame/copper rest, light sand frame/misty purple rest; colors do not change size or add designs.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
