## Printing and assembly

- A narrow middle compartment holds small long-handled tools, with wide end compartments for matching accessories; each end can be segmented separately, and the round ends make it easy to take items along the edge.
- One-piece, printed flat on its base; before use, check the middle compartment clear width and the clear length between the two end dividers.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
