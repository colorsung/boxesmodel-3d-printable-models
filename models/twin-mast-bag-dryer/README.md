## Intended use

- Each opened bag needs at least 94×64 mm internally, not measured flat. Suggested internal length ≤149 mm, with at least 20 mm above the rim. Keep adjacent spaces clear; recheck stability if raising posts for longer bags.
- Empty water promptly. Posts do not lock; carry by the tray and avoid wind or impacts. Check bag fit, moisture resistance, leakage, hygiene and drying; a filament name is not food-contact certification.

## Printing and assembly

- Complete printed parts: 1 drip base tray and 4 domed posts, 5 in total; no axles, glue or hardware to buy.
- Each bag's clear inner width and depth at least 94 × 64 mm; open the bag mouth and measure it first; do not use only the bag's flat width. Each position may take up the total outer width of 166 mm and 130 mm front to back; bags must not intrude into neighboring positions.
- The effective height from post top to tray rim is 169 mm; keep the bag's inner length at or below 149 mm so the bag mouth is at least 20 mm from the rim; longer bags need more height and a stability check.
- 0.4 mm single-side clearance in the post holes, 21 mm insertion, posts supported at the hole bottom; insert each post vertically all the way down, then pull the bag mouth down over the four posts with the bag bottom touching the domes.
- Lift bags out first, then remove posts one by one to clean the drip tray; pour out water in the tray promptly. The joints do not lock; carry it by the base tray and avoid wind or bumps.
- Print the base tray flat on its base with the posts upright. Before use, check slicing, a test print, the fit of your specific bags, moisture resistance, leak resistance, hygiene and drying efficiency; use a material suitable for cleaning; do not take a filament name as food contact certification.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
