## Printing and assembly

- 1 complete printed part, a one-piece tray; no assembly hardware or glue needed. Each position holds one complete original package, keeping the label and expiry date visible.
- Each position's top opening is 76 × 124 mm; recommended flat blister edge outline 74 × 122 mm; each flat edge must span the 4 mm ledge. Do not force undersized or oversized blister packs in or cut off the original package information.
- Recommended bulge projection no more than 66 × 114 mm, protruding downward no more than 20 mm. Bulges face down, with the flat edge resting on the shoulders; measure the actual complete package first; comparing outer box sizes alone is not enough.
- A 22 mm front finger notch lets you lift the flat rim from below, then take the whole plate out from the top. Each position is accessed independently and needs clearance above; do not stack several boards pressing on a bulge.
- This product only organizes complete original packaging, does not touch loose medication, and is not a child-safe box or medication reminder; medicines must still be stored as their original packaging requires.
- Print flat base down; open above the shoulders. Check slicing and test-print before printing, test-fit with your actual packaging, and first check burrs and the integrity of the support edges.
- Default soft purple, with cream, haze blue or light apricot options; colors do not indicate the order of taking medication.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
