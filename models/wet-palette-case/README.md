## Printing and assembly

- Supply a suitable absorbent sponge and palette paper; the paper length and width are also the sponge cut size; enter the sponge thickness as its actual working thickness when wet, with paper thickness set separately. Consumables are not included in the print files.
- Lay the wet sponge and flatten the palette paper first, then put the paper frame on the outer step; covers 4 mm of the paper on all four sides. Add a little water through the small holes on both sides; the reference line is measured from the inner tray floor and does not go above the sponge top; remove the frame to empty and wash it.
- Twin ears lift the frame straight up, and the four corner notches let it pass the lid locating pins; line the lid up with the four pins from above and drop it onto the tray rim. Lift the lid at least 3 mm before moving it sideways, and empty it before carrying; no latch; do not lift the whole box by the lid.
- The number, size and depth of the separate shallow wells on the lid are adjustable; put the lid on a nearby flat table in use. The wet tray work surface stays continuous, with no dividers on the paper.
- This model does not promise airtightness, leak resistance, moisture retention time or carrying with water; check for leaks and make sure the paper does not curl before use. Test PLA layer seams, material and paint compatibility and cleaning yourself.
- All three parts print flat side down; check the short bridges of the four 4.8 mm blind holes in the lid underside and the small locating pins when slicing. Test-print the 0.4 mm design fit first to check tightness and paper clamping force.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
