# Deep Cabinet Front-Pull Steady Bottle Box

A dedicated fingertip space behind the front grip, plus bottle column compartments and low side walls, makes it easy to load and unload from the front of a narrow, deep cabinet.

## Printing and assembly

- Each bottle compartment has 86.4 × 68.3 mm of straight-side clear space; recommended maximum jar diameter ≤ 66.3 mm, one jar per compartment. Holds only sealed packaged items.
- 26 mm of fingertip depth behind the front grip: first pull it straight out from the cabinet front, support the bottom, then lift the bottles out; measure bottle height and the gap to the shelf above with real items; the box height cannot substitute.
- High front and rear walls combine with low middle side walls, and a front divider stops bottle bases crowding the fingertip zone; the opening suits pulling with two fingers; it is not rated for lifting a full load with one hand.
- Only compares 0°/45° placement; the smaller square bed envelope is about 238.3 mm, plus skirt/brim and machine keep-out zones; not every long version fits a 256 mm heated bed.
- One-piece, base down; slice-check bridging or supports for the grip crossbar; before use, check the long side walls, handle strength and material heat deformation. Support the bottom when loading heavy items.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
