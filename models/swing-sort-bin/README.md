# Independent Swing Lid Sorting Box

Each compartment has its own central swing lid, the whole upper frame lifts off to empty, and the inner full-level line keeps clear of the lid rotation.

## Printing and assembly

- Supply an M4 through rod about 176 mm long, two washers and two nuts; hardware is not included in the print files or print envelope.
- Put each swing lid into the upper frame, align the axle holes, pass the axle rod in from the side and fix both ends with washers and nuts; keep 0.5–1 mm of axial play; do not clamp the swing lid. Then fit the upper frame over the box opening; lift the whole set to empty it.
- The horizontal line on the inner front wall marks full; keep material below it so it does not block the lower edge of the swing lid; for dry scraps on a desk; not a liquid, airtight or odor-proof container.
- The axle center sits above the lid flap's uniform-material center of gravity; return also depends on infill density and friction; test-print to check actual fill density, axle hole friction and return smoothness first.
- Box base down; choose orientation and supports in the slicer for the upper frame axle holes and the swing lid cross axle; test-print the hole sizes and fit first; support-free printing is not claimed.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
