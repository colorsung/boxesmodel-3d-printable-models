## Printing and assembly

- Each group's sliding lid pulls out separately from the front, with compartments inside set by use; the sliding lid has a rear stop but no anti-separation lock.
- A raised tab on the lid lets a fingertip push and pull it; keep at least one lid length of space in front of the box when in use, and support the lid once pulled out.
- Pack small items by day or task; not a sealed or child-resistant structure; not a guarantee of medicine storage conditions. Test-print to check track friction first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
