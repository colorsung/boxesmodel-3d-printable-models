## Intended use

- Measure the frame's bridge width, height and temple thickness. Trial-fit to ensure lenses remain clear and the lid does not press on the frame; add your own padding if needed.
- The lift-off lid has no latch or seal. Print its outer face down; trial-print to check fit and surface protection.

## Printing and assembly

- The frame's nose bridge rests on the middle bridge rest, with folded temples toward the rear slot; space is left on both sides of the lenses, with a side compartment for the lens cloth.
- Measure bridge width, bridge height and temple thickness on your actual glasses frame; try it to confirm the lenses hang free and the lid does not press the frame; you can add your own soft pad.
- The lift-off lid has no latch or seal; print the lid face down; test-print to check the real fit and surface protection first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
