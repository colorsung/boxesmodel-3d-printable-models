The four walls fold flat in two stages, corner sleeves join the unfolded panels, and a lift-out insert supports columns and rows.

## Printing and assembly

- The four walls use 1.75 mm filament as hinge pins: first align the short wall with the base holes, then insert through the holes left in the long-side base; the long walls go in last.
- Supply 2 filament pins of 140 mm for the short walls and 2 of 180 mm for the long walls; hole diameter 2.3 mm, pin ends flush with the outer edge. No axle end lock; check that the pins are secure before use.
- Unfold the long walls, then the short walls; drop the four corner sleeves in from above, then put in the divided insert. Corner sleeves bridge neighboring wall panels with no snap fits; do not turn it upside down or carry it by the corner sleeves.
- Empty the box and remove the divided insert and four corner sleeves before folding; fold the two short walls first, then the two long walls. The folded preview lays removed parts on the right; they are not stored in the box.
- Choose the insert's clear cell size and effective depth for your items; with the insert off, its columns, rows and height do not affect the model structure. There are gaps at the hinges and wall joints; without an insert it only suits items that will not fall through the gaps.
- The base tray moves horizontally; no sealing, automatic locking, compatibility with similar models or stacking load is claimed. Folded height is for the body only, excluding the removed insert and corner sleeves.
- Exported in the display pose with each part's lowest point at 0; when slicing, put the large outer face of the wall plates and the top face of the corner sleeves on the bed. Check supports for the hole bridges/axle seat overhangs.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
