## Intended use

- Reference round tokens: diameter ≤26 mm, thickness ≤3 mm. Only the overhead view is blocked; angled views, lighting or transparent materials may reveal contents. No random-mixing or fairness claim.
- Hold the lid and slider down when mixing; never carry by the lid. The loose lid has no latch or seal, and the slider can exit sideways. Check hand access, fit, retention and spilling in use.

## Printing and assembly

- Complete print list: 1 closed-bottom cup body, 1 cap with integrated cover plate/brace/skirt/slide rails and 1 small-mouth slider, 3 printed parts in total. No axles, pins, screws, links, magnets or glue to buy; all manufactured parts download in the same export.
- First thread the slider into the double rails from the left or right of the cap, then drop the cap skirt into the cup mouth from the top. 0.4 mm nominal single-side clearance; the slider is stopped from lifting by the rail top edges but can still pull fully out sideways. The cap is a loose fit, not a latch or sealed lid.
- Reference round tokens ≤ 26 mm in diameter and ≤ 3 mm thick; with the slider open, two fingers come down through the front opening onto the cover plate, cross back past its end and reach down; after taking items, reverse: rise out of the rear zone, move forward into the opening and withdraw upward.
- 45 mm of wrap-around height above the cover plate, which extends 8 mm behind the opening; checked against a 42 × max(30, token diameter + 2) × 28 mm reference grip envelope. Only verified that the top-down view hides the bottom; blind draws are not guaranteed for oblique views, lighting or transparent materials.
- When loading/cleaning, you can pull off the whole cap first; an undivided loose-parts cavity is kept at the bottom. Hold the cap and slider down when mixing; do not carry it by the lid. Shaking randomness or fair draws are not guaranteed; check that it stays tight and does not spill in transport before use.
- Cup body base down, slider flat face down; flip the cap in the slicer so its large rear flat face is on the bed, and check slide rail bridging and cover plate supports. or verify real hand comfort and tolerance fit.
- Suggested colors: plum/mint, navy/apricot, grass green/lemon, off-white/clay, berry/haze blue; preview colors are for reference only and do not add models or printed parts.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
