## Printing and assembly

- Store only individually wrapped tea bags or coffee sticks; do not store loose tea or unwrapped food directly; check the clear compartment against the full package shape, then leave 1 mm per side.
- Lift the lid straight up; the locating ring under the lid fits into the box body; fixed low dividers for sorting; not removable.
- Complete set of 2 printed parts: compartment box body and flat lid with integrated locating ring; no purchased parts.
- Box body upright; flip the lid plate and place it on the build plate separately; no promise of sealing, moisture protection, food-contact safety or verified physical fit.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
