# Round Lift-Out Draining Caddy with Spout

A lift-out round basket separates from a continuously sloping base. Ring-arranged drains clear the space below, and a projecting spout points into the sink.

## Printing and assembly

- Two complete printed parts: a perforated compartment basket and a spouted base. No purchased fittings are required.
- The basket locating section inserts 4 mm into the base; its outer flange rests on the base rim. Lift it vertically to rinse the parts separately.
- The spout must project over the sink interior while the rest of the base sits on a level counter. Do not place it in the middle of a closed countertop where discharge would wet the surface.
- For wet sponges and cleaning brushes. Choose an open interior or two or four shared-wall compartments. Drainage rate, residual water and cleaning performance have not been physically tested.
- Slice both parts separately with bases down and check supports beneath the basket flange. No food-contact use is claimed.
- Preview colors are not included in STL/STEP.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
