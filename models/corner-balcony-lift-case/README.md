## Printing and assembly

- Print the outer box flat on its base; the raised inner part has four feet and an overhanging tray bottom; with feet down by default you must add removable supports under the tray bottom and clear them before assembly. No hardware to buy; the top edge stands 6 mm proud, the four feet rest on the outer box floor, and it lifts out vertically; transport locking or heavy loads are not promised.
- Units are millimeters; matching parts use files generated in the same export. Set clearance by your items' maximum size and leave room to take them in and out.
- Check orientation and supports before printing, and test-print key mating parts first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
