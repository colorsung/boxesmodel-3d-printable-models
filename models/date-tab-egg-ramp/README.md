## Printing and assembly

- Complete printed parts list: 1 closed-bottom egg queue slope rack and 1 date label frame; supply 1 date label, 32 × 14 mm, ≤ 0.4 mm thick. No axles, pins, screws or glue; the paper label is not in the STL/STEP.
- Use reference eggs from the same batch, oriented the same way across; maximum envelope 62 long × 44 across, nominal geometric queue of 5. The ellipsoid is only a stated assumption; real eggs or egg sizes are not guaranteed to fit. Refill eggs at the tail from the top, and take from the front first.
- To take the first egg: lift it straight up 9 mm, then move it out forward horizontally; the remaining eggs can roll along the 4° straight slope to fill gaps. Do not force eggs that are stuck sideways; check actual gravity rolling, friction and breakage risk before use.
- The date frame drops into the rear wall's blind slot from above; the locating tongue inserts 6 mm effectively with 0.4 mm single-side clearance; the bottom shoulder rests on top of the rear wall. Slide out the label frame first, then change the label, inserting the paper from above down to the bottom edge; paper slot thickness 0.8 mm.
- Rack base down; in the slicer, the date frame can be rotated so the large rear flat face is on the plate; check slot bridging and front edge supports. The exported manufacturing coordinates use the in-use orientation; choose the print orientation in your slicer.
- The full straight slope has no compartments, since dividers would block the queue. The outer envelope in use includes the label frame but not the eggs; check the fridge's actual shelf height, desk stability and bump protection. Before use, check food-contact materials, freshness, real load and cleaning durability.
- Suggested colors: sage/cream, haze blue/lemon, apricot/clay, grass green/charcoal, light purple/peach. Suggested colors do not change the number of manufactured parts.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.

### Additional hardware

Supply 1 date paper label separately (width and height per the current configuration, default 32×14 mm, thickness ≤0.4 mm). The print files contain 1 ramp frame body and 1 date label frame; no shafts, pins, screws or glue.
