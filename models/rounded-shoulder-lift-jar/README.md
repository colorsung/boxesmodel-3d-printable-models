## Printing and assembly

- Insert items from above. Key dimensions describe conservative straight-through clearance; rounded corners, dividers and shaped ends also occupy space. Allow handling clearance for the widest part of each item.
- Two parts are included: the jar body and lid. The locating ring inserts by 4 mm, the plate rests on the rim and the grip lifts upward. The curved interior uses a conservative radial inset, so local thickness is not uniform.
- Clearances describe conservative rectangular regions accessible vertically to the floor. Check items in each enabled compartment; the widest outer diameter is not the entrance or compartment diameter.
- Place the jar base down; choose lid orientation and supports in the slicer. The lid is neither sealed nor latched.
- Nominal ring clearance at the straight neck is 0.45 mm per side; test the ring fit before printing the full kit.
- All custom parts are included in the download; no purchased parts are required. Preview colors are for display only; manufacturing STL/STEP files are uncolored.
- Check overhangs, supports and fit before printing; support-free printing, load capacity, sealing and food-contact safety are not promised.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
