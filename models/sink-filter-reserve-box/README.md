## Intended use

- Only dry unused filters; check maximum diameter against the narrowest cavity minus 2 mm handling allowance, not brand. No automatic bottom dispensing or water seal at the cutout.
- The reference states slicing and test printing have not been done; digital geometry checks do not establish physical fit.

## Printing and assembly

- 2 printed parts: 1 box body and 1 lid with an integrated grip; no required purchased parts.
- The box body and lid are each printed separately, flat floor down. The lid drops onto the ledge from above and lifts straight up; no snap fits and no hidden pins.
- The wall-thickness parameter sets the floor and lid thickness; the main compartment's straight walls are twice that, and the lid ledge is about the wall thickness minus the fit clearance wide.
- To take one out, lift the lid first, then pinch the strainer at the front cutout and lift it out; not a bottom auto-dispensing structure.
- The suggested maximum outer diameter is the narrowest clear cavity minus 2 mm of pick-up room; compatibility with every brand is not promised. The cutout is not waterproof or sealed; store only dry, unused strainers.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
