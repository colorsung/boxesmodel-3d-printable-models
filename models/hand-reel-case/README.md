## Intended use

- Size the spool for the maker's bend radius. Not for powered high-load cables, fiber optics or fragile cables. No spring rewind, lock or seal; check capacity, friction and movement in use.
- Supply six M3 screws and six DIN934 nuts, 5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thick. Screw heads: diameter ≤6 mm, height ≤3 mm. Choose length for the stack without protruding tips.
- Deburr outlets and slots; check hardware retention and the 0.4 mm rotation clearance. Keep connectors outside the winding cavity.

## Printing and assembly

- Four separate parts; supply 6 M3 screws and 6 DIN 934 nuts (nominal 5.5 mm across flats, 2.4 mm thick). Screw head outer diameter ≤ 6 mm, height ≤ 3 mm; choose length by the current stack size; the tail must not protrude from the bottom.
- First fit 2 nuts from the underside of the reel core, lay the middle of the cable in the through slot and lead the two ends out in opposite directions; align the two holes of the upper stop plate and fix it with 2 M3 screws.
- Fit the reel core over the base shell's locating post, drop the cable into the top openings on both sides, and leave the connectors outside; the ring lid slips down over the handle, aligns with the four corner holes and is fixed with 4 sets of M3 screws/nuts.
- Turn the handle to wind the cable, leaving slack at both ends; pull both ends to let out cable. To take it apart, remove the four corner screws and the ring lid, then lift the reel core; no spring rewind, locking or sealing mechanism.
- Choose the reel core by the cable maker's minimum bend radius; not for live high-load cables, optical fiber or fragile cables. Connectors do not enter the winding cavity; before use, check the actual cable length capacity, smoothness and friction.
- The base shell, reel core, upper stop plate and ring lid each print flat side down; check the blind hole and nut pocket bridges when slicing. Deburr the cable exit and slot edges, and check hardware loosening and the 0.4 mm rotation clearance.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
