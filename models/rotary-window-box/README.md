## Intended use

- Dry beads, screws and small parts; no indexing stops, and the window may span neighboring cells during rotation. Lid clearance means no anti-mixing, sealing or transport-locking claim.
- Supply an M3 screw, hex nut and 12 mm outer-diameter washer. Choose screw length for the height without protruding tips; seat the washer on the shoulder and check friction and retention.

## Printing and assembly

- First put the M3 hex nut into the bottom recess, fit the rotating lid, then fit the M3 screw and a 12 mm outer diameter large washer from above; supply your own hardware.
- The washer presses on the central axle shoulder, with 0.4 mm of axial clearance between the lid and washer; choose screw length by the current height; the tail must not protrude from the bottom.
- Turn the lid window over the compartment center to take items; turn to the solid sector to cover the opening. No detents; while turning, the window may span two neighboring compartments.
- The storage compartment count excludes the solid closed sector; compartment count, depth and window proportion change the model structure; the selected compartment only changes the assembly display.
- For dry small items such as beads and screws; there is clearance under the lid; no mixing prevention, sealing or transport locking is promised. Remove the screws to lift the whole lid off for cleaning.
- Print the base box bottom and the rotating lid flat face down; check the axle shoulder, rotation friction and hardware loosening before use.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
