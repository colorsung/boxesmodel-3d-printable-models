## Printing and assembly

- Needs 8 matching disc magnets supplied separately; 0.2 mm per side and 0.2 mm extra depth in the holes for gluing; dry-fit first and confirm the attracting polarity top and bottom.
- The four corner magnet posts occupy the corner cells; row and column cells are fixed and printed in one piece. Lift the lid off; the outer skirt stops sideways movement, and a front notch lets your thumb hold the box.
- Box base down; flip the box lid 180° in the slicer so the flat face is down and the magnet holes face up.
- For desk sorting only; there is a design gap between the dividers and the lid; sealing or keeping cells separate when inverted is not guaranteed. Test magnet gluing, pull force and real fit; no child protection is provided.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
