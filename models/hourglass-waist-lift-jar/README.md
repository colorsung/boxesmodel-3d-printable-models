## Printing and assembly

- Place items in the jar, then lower the lid ring vertically into the opening. Lift the rounded top grip to remove the lid. Nominal ring clearance is 0.45 mm per side, with 4 mm insertion depth.
- Print the body with its base down; choose the lid orientation and supports in the slicer. Both parts are supplied separately; the ring is neither a seal nor a latch.
- The widest part of an item must pass through the waist with handling clearance. Enabled dividers further reduce space; the clearance diameter is not the usable diameter of each compartment.
- All custom parts are included in the download; no purchased parts are required. Preview colors are for display only; manufacturing STL/STEP files are uncolored.
- Check overhangs, supports and fit before printing; support-free printing, load capacity, sealing and food-contact safety are not promised.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
