## Printing and assembly

- First lift the box above the mirror base, pass the opening around the thin rod from the side, then lower it straight onto the base; the clearance diameter must be larger than the base diameter. Do not pass a full round base sideways straight through the narrow opening.
- One-piece, printed flat on its base; the arc cells share walls; the wraparound outline does not clamp the mirror base. Outer length and width control the actual envelope; the opening angle affects how far it wraps around.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
