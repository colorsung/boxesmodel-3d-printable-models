## Printing and assembly

- Cylinders go in sideways, and each column refills from the top; enter the measured total length including the nub and the maximum outer diameter; keep the same size and direction within a column.
- Gently support the whole column from below and lift about 2.2 mm, take the bottom piece forward, then lower the rest slowly; a lip at the bottom opening stops items rolling straight out.
- Solid walls between columns and a wide base keep the columns apart; capacity already leaves 4 mm at the top; test-print to check actual smoothness and stability first; automatic dispensing is not promised.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
