## Printing and assembly

- 2 printed parts in the set: 1 wide drip basin and 1 vented tube with feet. No required purchased hardware, glue or custom machined parts; both parts use the same set of parameters.
- For short folding umbrellas up to 58 mm in diameter when folded, standing upright; tube inner diameter 62 mm, 2 mm on each side. The umbrella tip rests on crossed ribs 27.5 mm above the outer base, with an insertion depth of 162.5 mm in the tube.
- Close the umbrella fabric and shake off most of the water, then put it in tip down from the top; hold the basin and lift the umbrella straight up to take it. Wrap the umbrella strap and keep it inside the tube, not hanging by the window. Only for short folding umbrellas; not for long umbrellas, walking sticks or load-bearing support.
- During assembly the tube feet drop from the top into the four-segment locating ring with 0.5 mm radial clearance, and the four curved feet touch the basin floor. No latch; grip the complete upper ring to lift it straight out; take out the umbrella before lifting the tube, emptying the basin and washing.
- Four 5 mm diameter drain holes in the tube bottom; crossed ribs raise the umbrella tip 2 mm; the tube bottom is 0.5 mm above the basin rim. Side windows ventilate, and the locating ring and tube feet each have four 12 mm drainage openings; empty the water promptly; check actual leak resistance and drainage speed before use.
- The basin's clear water depth is 19 mm, not counting the tube feet; this is not a capacity promise. Empty rack outer envelope 140 × 130 × 190 mm, excluding the umbrella and lifting height; for umbrellas that are too tall or heavy, do not judge stability by diameter alone.
- Basin bottom down; with the tube feet down, the round bottom and upper ring overhang; check supports when slicing and clear them through the side windows. Support-free printing is not promised. Before use, test-print to check leak resistance, load, tip resistance and long-term tolerance of heat and moisture.
- Optional haze blue tube with a warm white basin, ochre tube with a light sand basin, or gray-green tube with a misty purple basin; the existing preview uses the template's main color.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
