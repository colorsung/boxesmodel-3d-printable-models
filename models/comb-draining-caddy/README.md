## Printing and assembly

- Combs go into the narrow upper compartment, and bottom holes lead water into the drip tray below; remove the combs before washing.
- The drip tray pulls fully forward; usable water depth is below the notch, so empty it promptly. Check printed parts for leak resistance and hygiene before use.
- Check the narrow compartment floor bridge, drain holes and stand orientation when slicing.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
