## Printing and assembly

- The central tall cup holds pens or long-handled tools; low outer compartments sort small parts, with low edges for easy pinching.
- One-piece, printed flat on its base; the central and outer floors are joined; outer length and width are the octagon's outer envelope.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
