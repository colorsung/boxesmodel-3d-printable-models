## Printing and assembly

- The paper label inserts from above into a 1.2 mm deep slot; the label paper should be 0.4 mm narrower than the label slot and as tall as the slot; paper is not included in the print files.
- The outer width includes the 6 mm front label frame; compartments are calculated from the remaining interior space.
- Print base down; the top edge of the label window has a bridge; check local supports when slicing.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
