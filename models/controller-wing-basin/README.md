## Intended use

- For disconnected small game controllers and wrist straps. Desktop placement only, without clamping; do not assume compatibility by model name.
- Allow handling clearance around maximum item dimensions. One piece, no hardware; check orientation and supports, and trial-print critical fits.

## Printing and assembly

- One-piece, no hardware. The handle drops into the angled wings from above, with sticks and buttons facing up; for returning items on the desk only; does not clamp devices.
- Units are millimeters; matching parts use files generated in the same export. Set clearance by your items' maximum size and leave room to take them in and out.
- Check orientation and supports before printing, and test-print key mating parts first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
