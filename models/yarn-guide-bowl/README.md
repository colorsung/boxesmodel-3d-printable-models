## Intended use

- One free-moving yarn ball; use maximum measured diameter and clearance per side. Remove supports and burrs, then check snagging with actual yarn.
- Check moving feed, friction and stability under tension in use; no tipping resistance or forceful pull-out claim.

## Printing and assembly

- Set by the yarn ball's maximum measured diameter; movement clearance is the distance per side. The single yarn ball space stays open, with no dividers to block its movement.
- Lay the yarn sideways into the right slot from the top edge, then wrap it along the return hook to the bottom guide point; no need to cut the yarn or punch holes in the knitted piece. To remove the cable, slide it out along the slot in reverse.
- The guide slot's nominal width is the cord diameter + 1.5 mm, with 0.5 mm edge chamfers; after printing, remove supports and burrs and check for snagging with real yarn.
- Check slicing with the flat base down; the top edge of the return hook may need supports; before use, check how a free yarn ball pays out, friction, tension stability and snagging; not a guarantee against tipping or forceful pulling off.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
