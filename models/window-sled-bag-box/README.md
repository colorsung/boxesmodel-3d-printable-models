Stack clean food storage bags, slide off the lid to refill and pinch edges through the top window; size the box and window for stacks and drawer height.

## Intended use

- Clean food storage or resealable bags; suggested folded stack ≤230×100×58.2 mm. Measure zipper bulk and stack thickness, not nominal capacity. Flexible bags need bending and manual separation.
- For horizontal drawer storage only, without transport lock or seal. No heavy objects on the lid; leave 236.2 mm ahead for removal. No automatic separation; check warping and sliding with a test print.

## Printing and assembly

- 2 complete printed parts: 1 side-rail closed-bottom box and 1 window sliding lid; no hardware, magnets or glue to buy. The whole set uses the same parameters.
- Recommended flat stacked envelope for clean food bags up to 230 × 100 × 58.2 mm; measure the folded zipper and stack thickness first; do not judge by the bag's nominal capacity.
- Pull window clear opening 100 × 35 mm, with a separate finger hole at the front. Hold the box and pinch the bag edge through the window; soft bags need bending; automatic single-sheet separation is not guaranteed.
- Push the sliding lid in horizontally from the front slot to the rear wall; 0.4 mm per side sideways and vertically; side slots engage 0.8 mm deep. Slide out the whole lid to refill or clean, leaving 236.2 mm of exit space in front.
- This box is for horizontal use inside a drawer, with no transport locking or sealing. Do not stack heavy items on the lid.
- The box and lid plates are both zeroed flat side down; verify the overhangs of the box mouth and inner rails and the flatness of the lid plates with slicing and a clearance test piece. Check slicing and test-print before printing; before use, check long-term warping and actual pulling.
- Default haze blue box with a light sand lid; optional sage green with cream, or warm gray with coral orange. Preview colors do not affect manufacturing geometry.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
