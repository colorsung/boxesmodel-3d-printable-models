## Printing and assembly

- All six parts are printed, including two clamping rods and two protective pads; no screws need to be bought. The front of the box is open cells, with the desk board behind the clamp seat.
- First screw the two threaded rods in from under the clamp seat without tightening. Push the clamp seat onto the desk edge horizontally, with the upper arm against the desktop; place the protective pads recess down on the threaded rod tips, then tighten the two rods alternately and lightly until the pads touch the desk underside.
- The protective pad is removable and has no clips; support it by hand when loosening the screws so it does not fall. Tighten lightly to position only; do not use tools to add force.
- After fitting the clamp seat, insert the two T-keys on the back of the box from above at the same time until they rest on the slot bottoms. To take out a box, empty it first, lift it straight up at least 39 mm, then move it forward; leave enough room above to take it out.
- Only suits desk boards that are flat top and bottom with enough edge clearance; the desk board is not included in the print files. Under-desk beams, skirts, rounded or beveled edges need measuring separately; scaling cannot replace a proper fit.
- Test-print to check the 0.35 mm nominal thread clearance and 0.4 mm T-slot clearance first. Before use, check clamping force, load-bearing, layer strength, creep, slip resistance and desk marks; test-fit empty first, then try it with light small items.
- Box bottom down with the screw axes vertical; the clamp seat lies on its side to check layer direction and supports; slice the protective pad with the recesses up.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
