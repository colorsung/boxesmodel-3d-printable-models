## Printing and assembly

- 3 parts: lower box, shallow upper tray, and lift-off lid; place the tray on the internal ledge, then fit the lid. Remove the parts in reverse order; 6 mm of grip space is left above the tray rim.
- The block weave pattern uses shallow grooves; the remaining wall is thicker than the specified base wall thickness; remove the upper tray before accessing items below.
- All required printed parts are included; no purchased hardware is needed. Preview colors are for display only; the STL/STEP manufacturing files contain no color.
- Test the fit clearance with a sample on your printer and material first; sealing, leak-proofing, load capacity, and food-contact safety are not guaranteed.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
