## Intended use

- For temporary storage of dry sponges and covers only; bottoms are closed. No disinfection, drying or hygiene-performance claim.
- Allow handling clearance around the largest item dimensions. One piece, no hardware; check print orientation and supports, and trial-print critical fits.

## Printing and assembly

- One-piece, no hardware. Temporarily holds only dried items; the compartment floors are closed; take out from above; no disinfection, drying or hygiene performance is claimed.
- Units are millimeters; matching parts use files generated in the same export. Set clearance by your items' maximum size and leave room to take them in and out.
- Check orientation and supports before printing, and test-print key mating parts first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
