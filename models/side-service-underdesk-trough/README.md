## Printing and assembly

- The printed kit contains one trough. Mounting flanges are 4 mm thick, giving a nominal 12 mm wood penetration for 16 mm screws. Screws are not included as STL/STEP parts.
- Hold all four flange tops against the desk underside, mark the holes and drive screws from below. Support the trough before undoing the screws.
- Feed complete plugs through the open ends and access cable bundles through the side opening. For light USB and data cables, not as a load-bearing power-strip or power-supply mount.
- Slice with the flat base down and check supports for the mounting-flange overhangs. Desktop fixing strength has not been validated.
- Preview colors are not included in STL/STEP.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.

### Additional hardware

Four 3.5 × 16 mm cross-recess pan-head chipboard screws, head diameter at most 7 mm. For drillable wooden desktops at least 20 mm thick; check the substrate and pilot holes first.
