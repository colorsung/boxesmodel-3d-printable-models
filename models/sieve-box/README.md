## Printing and assembly

- The upper level's rows of holes go all the way through, and the closed base tray catches what falls; the basket rests on a continuous support ledge with a fixed 0.4 mm single-side gap.
- Suits sorting and drying parts; no food contact or waterproof sealing is promised. Both parts base down; test-print to check the real fit first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
