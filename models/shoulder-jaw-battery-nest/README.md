# Shoulder-Clamp Power Tool Battery Dock

Size the stationary holder to battery shoulders. Remove four screws and both jaws to lift out; the battery button does not release it. Jaws do not squeeze.

## Intended use

- Only for measured flat-bottom housings with projecting shoulders on both sides. Measure shoulders and upper offsets; keep contacts, vents and release buttons clear. No brand or Ah compatibility claim.
- Provide 4 M3 nuts (across flats ≤5.5 mm, height ≤2.4 mm) and 4 M3×20 screws (head diameter ≤6.2 mm, height ≤3 mm). Screw ends must not extend below the feet.
- Static storage only, without contacts, charging, magnets or system interfaces; not a substitute for transport restraint. Check slicing, heat resistance, jaw strength, drop retention and actual fit.

## Printing and assembly

- Only suits measured housings with a flat bottom and protruding shoulders on both sides. Measure the shoulder length, width, height and the upper inset on each side; the clip strip area must not cover terminals, vents or the release button; no brand or Ah compatibility is promised.
- First fit 4 M3 nuts (AF ≤ 5.5 mm, height ≤ 2.4 mm) from below, seat the battery on top, then place the left and right clip strips; fit 4 M3×20 screws from above (head Ø ≤ 6.2 mm, height ≤ 3 mm). A thin tool holds the nut from between the feet, with the tail end not past the feet.
- The clip strips have 0.4 mm design clearance to the shoulder tops and 0.6 mm per side at the top; held by geometric capture rather than compressing the battery. To take out the battery, back out 4 screws and lift the two clip strips; the battery's own button does not release this frame.
- Static storage only; no electrical contacts, charging, magnets or external system interfaces. Print parts flat; slice-check the nut cavities, posts and counterbores; before use, check heat resistance, clip strip strength, drop/transport retention and the actual fit.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
