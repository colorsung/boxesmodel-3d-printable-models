## Printing and assembly

- One-piece print; capsule flanges rest on the shoulders on both sides, slide along the long slot to the front finger pocket and lift out.
- Holds only fully sealed, cold capsules; the clear diameter must include packaging edge tolerance; fit with every brand is not guaranteed.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
