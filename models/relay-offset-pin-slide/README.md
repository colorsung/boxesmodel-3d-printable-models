## Printing and assembly

- Holds only disconnected, cold, unplugged spare parts; not an electrical enclosure; anti-static, insulation, waterproofing and heat protection are not promised.
- 2 parts in the set: box and sliding lid; no hardware. Print the lid flat and the box base down; set supports for the rail top lips in the slicer and clean them off.
- The lid slides in from the left end along the length and stops at the right end wall; the upper lip limits lifting. To open, pull the whole lid out to the left, then take items from above.
- Single-side clearance is set separately; no clip, seal or transport anti-opening promise.
- Units are millimeters; matching parts use files generated in the same export. Set clearance by your items' maximum size and leave room to take them in and out.
- Check orientation and supports before printing, and test-print key mating parts first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
