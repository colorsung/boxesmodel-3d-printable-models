## Printing and assembly

- The rotating tray fits vertically over the base axle and is supported by the ring-shaped bottom support face; lifts out for washing; no latch.
- Spread light loads evenly; turning needs a full circle of clear desk space; test-print to check surface friction and tipping stability first.
- Below the central compartment is a closed axle seat; check axle hole bridging and contact surface flatness when slicing.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
