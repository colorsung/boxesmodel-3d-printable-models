## Printing and assembly

- Height is the total including the handle; basket height alone sets the storage depth.
- The one-piece handle is at least 4 mm thick and separates the left and right zones; not a living hinge.
- Print base down; configure supports in the slicer for the top edge of the handle cut-out; check the real load-bearing before use.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
