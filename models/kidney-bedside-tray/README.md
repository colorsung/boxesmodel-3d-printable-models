## Printing and assembly

- The long compartment holds glasses or a remote, with small cells at the left end for keys and the like; a recessed front leaves room for fingers to reach long items.
- One-piece, printed flat on its base. Outer length and width control the actual envelope; check the recessed clear compartments against your measured items.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
