## Printing and assembly

- Maximum envelope of the inserted body section 24.4 × 21.4 mm; the sleeve is set to 26 × 23 mm clear width/depth; the body's bottom needs flat shoulders to rest on; fit is not inferred from brand or a curved bottom.
- The original straight plug's maximum envelope is 14 × 10 × 14 mm, with another 0.8 mm left in the central hole; connect the cables by hand first, then seat it straight down from the top; the weight rests on the shoulders on both sides, and a 4 mm cable drops through the back slot and exits along the rear opening.
- The cable's minimum bend radius is set to 6 mm (the original cable's requirements still apply); the shoulder top is 30 mm high, and the rear-open cavity under the deck can take a complete plug. The seat does not fix or auto-connect plugs and contains no circuits; not for side-entry/L-shaped plugs or unmeasured devices.
- 2 front accessory compartments for clean, dry capped accessories, loaded and lifted from the top; the plug lifts straight out with the body; unplug before maintenance. For flat, dry countertops; before use, check friction and load-bearing; anti-slip feet can be added.
- One-piece, no assembly hardware. Base on the plate; the shoulders form cross bridges, so check bridging/supports and how to remove them when slicing; support-free printing is not claimed. Before use, check actual stability, material, electrical fit and temperature rise.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
