## Intended use

- Only similarly sized dry craft granules, not powder, sharp or sticky items. Stop and empty jams; never force. No exact-count, food-contact or sealing claim; check bridging, shear jams and loaded stability.
- Supply four M3 socket-head screws, four DIN934 hex nuts and a 2.5 mm tool; select length from the current list, default M3×40 mm. No countersunk/shoulder screws or tall locknuts; no washers needed.
- Check full thread engagement without protruding tips; do not thread into plastic or overtighten. Empty and remove the plate before disassembly. Test fit with a print and check empty rotation before loading.

## Printing and assembly

- The knob's raised side shows the pick-up cavity: facing up it collects, a half turn down it dispenses; the catch tray pulls out horizontally from the knob side.
- Only for dry craft granules of similar measured size; do not add powders, sharp or sticky items. If it jams while turning, stop and empty it; do not force it.
- Purchased hardware: 4 × M3×40 mm socket head cap screws (DIN 912/ISO 4762, coarse 0.5 mm, length measured under the head, at least 8 mm of thread at the end) and 4 × M3 hex nuts (DIN 934, 5.5 mm across flats, 2.4 mm thick); the tool is a 2.5 mm hex key.
- The preview and STL/STEP contain only the 5 printed parts; metal screws, nuts and tools must be supplied separately. Choose by the current model size; do not use countersunk, captive shoulder screws or tall lock nuts; no extra washers needed.
- Installation ① Empty the storage compartment, put the drum in from the knob side, then pass the end plate's center hole over the knob with the four corner holes aligned; bosses face outward, with the ring on the inside of the end plate facing the shell.
- Installation ② Press the four nuts into the hex slots from the back of the shell until they reach the inner end steps; you can lay it back side up and drop the nuts in one by one, steadying them by hand; the nuts do not lock up before the screws are tightened.
- Installation ③ Insert the screws from outside the four bosses and tighten diagonally and evenly with a hex key until the end plate sits flush. Load path: screw head → integrated boss/end plate → shell → hex nut; do not drive screws into plastic threads.
- Installation ④ Check that the nuts sit against the shoulders and the screw heads against the bosses, with the nominal tail 1 mm inside the back and 1 mm past the nut; still check with real parts that it is fully engaged and does not protrude. Once a half turn runs smoothly empty, fit the catch tray, add material and close the top lid; do not overtighten and crush the plastic.
- The end plate must be removed to take out the drum; empty the storage compartment before assembling or disassembling. The 0.4 mm radial/axial clearance is a design starting point; test-print to check the actual fit first.
- Rotation transfers material from a single cavity; check actual dispensing amount, piling and bridging, shear jamming, friction and full-compartment stability before use; exact counts are not promised.
- Keep the shell base down; check supports for the inner tube top and the funnel; slice the knob/end plate lying flat along the axis. No food contact or sealing is claimed.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
