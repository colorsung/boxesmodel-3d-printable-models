## Printing and assembly

- The fixed rear brace is generated for a 22 mm monitor housing top thickness and a back that flares out 15° from vertical going down; not a living hinge. Only for monitor housings whose two rear foot contact areas have the same straight back profile.
- The front lip reaches down 6 mm; the front bezel's effective non-display height must be at least 7 mm. The monitor housing's effective width must be at least the 200 mm tray length, and the front lip must not touch the display, buttons or sensors.
- Drop from above onto two 14 mm wide ledges; 8 mm gap from the top to the tray bottom, with 126 mm clear between the two ledges. First check that the vent holes are not covered by the ledges/rear feet; check actual heat dissipation before use.
- One-piece with no hardware; install empty first, and empty it before lifting it out whole. The front lip and rear feet only rest against the housing for location; do not force them on, clamp the screen or rely on bending to fit.
- For sorting light small items such as USB drives; do not judge load-bearing from the preview. Housing loads, scratches, tip resistance, long-term creep and temperature rise have not been measured; fit is not claimed for unknown curved backs, bumps or narrow bezels.
- For printing on its side, check print orientation and overhang supports; the model's outer height includes the rear brace; the main tray's usable clear height excludes the floor thickness.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
