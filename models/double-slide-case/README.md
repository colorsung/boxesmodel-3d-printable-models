## Printing and assembly

- The two lids slide out toward the left and right short sides, and a full-height central wall keeps the sides separate.
- Print the box and both lids flat base down separately; check bridging or local supports for the rail top edges when slicing.
- Fixed 0.4 mm single-side design gap; fits only the box from the same set; no latch, seal or inverted cell separation is guaranteed.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
