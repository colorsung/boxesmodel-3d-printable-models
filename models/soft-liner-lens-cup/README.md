## Intended use

- Default maximum diameter including switches is ≤70 mm, with 1 mm side clearance and a 2.4 mm bottom and side liner. Do not support exposed glass or a protruding bare mount face down.
- Print the rigid holder and TPU cup separately. Trial-print tolerances and soft material; do not force a tight fit. Elasticity, shrinkage, scratching and tall-lens tipping have no physical validation.
- No lens clamping, drop protection, dust protection or broad brand-compatibility claim. Remove the lens before the cup; never lift a loaded holder by its liner.

## Printing and assembly

- Lenses must stand upright with a rear cap on, maximum diameter including the dial ≤ 70 mm; 1 mm clear per side; the bottom and side contact faces are isolated by 2.4 mm soft liners. Bare lenses and protruding bare mounts must not face down onto supports.
- Print the rigid compartment and TPU soft cups flat side down separately; the soft cup sits on the compartment floor with a 0.4 mm per side sliding fit, its top 2 mm above the rigid compartment. Test-print tolerances and soft materials first; do not force tight matching parts in.
- Windows on both sides let you grip the lens barrel, and empty soft cups can be lifted out by the exposed edges; take the glasses out before removing the cup. Elasticity, shrinkage, scratching and tipping of tall lenses have not been measured; do not carry the base compartment with a lens by its soft liner.
- This is upright storage on a desk or fixed drawer; it does not clamp lenses and offers no drop protection, lens dust protection or universal brand fit.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
