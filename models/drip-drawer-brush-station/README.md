## Intended use

- Whole electric toothbrushes and original charging bases; measure maximum outlines, not brand. The divider does not establish electrical water protection; follow the original equipment's location requirements.
- Remove brushes before cleaning the tray and disconnect power and dry before service; check leakage, handle stability, water/cleaning resistance, electrical surroundings and fit.

## Printing and assembly

- Maximum full toothbrush outer diameter 31 mm, plus 0.6 mm radial allowance per seat; the bottom support surrounds 6 mm drain holes, and drips fall into a separate tray below.
- The original charging base is set by a maximum rectangular envelope of 48 × 60 mm, with 0.6 mm on each side; the cable exit opens upward at the back, so the plug does not need to pass through a small hole. Not a brand-specific fit chart.
- The drip tray pulls out horizontally straight forward; move the toothbrushes away first, then take the tray out to clean. The continuous middle wall stops water flowing across the seat, but that does not make it electrically waterproof; follow the original device's site requirements; unplug and dry before maintenance.
- Two parts; no added hardware or electrical components. Tray bottom down; check supports for the frame's high support bridges and how to remove them when slicing. Water collection depth is a structural size; check printed parts for leaks before use.
- Before use, check the stability of your actual brush handles, the material's water resistance and cleanability, the electrical environment and assembly tolerances.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
