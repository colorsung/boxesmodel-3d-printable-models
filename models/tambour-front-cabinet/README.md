## Intended use

- Cards or desktop items; keep the overhead return path empty. Outer size is not usable space. No seal or open/closed lock; do not fold or force slats. Check pins, tracks, nut retention and loads with a test print.
- Supply seven 1.75 mm filament pins, each 145 mm long, plus four M3×16 screws and M3 hex nuts, 5.5 mm across flats and about 2.4 mm thick. Remove bottom screws before the right panel.

## Printing and assembly

- Front-opening shutter cabinet: first link the slats with pins, put the chain into the left rail, close the right rail plate, and finally fix the 4 bottom hardware sets.
- Supply 7 pins of 1.75 mm filament, each 145 mm, flush with both ends of the guide axle; pin holes 2.2 mm; once the side plates are fitted they stop the pins sliding sideways.
- Supply 4 sets of M3×16 screws and M3 hex nuts (5.5 mm across flats, about 2.4 mm thick); put the nut in through the right window, lay the empty cabinet on its side and fix it with a screw from the bottom.
- Lift the front handle and the shutter slats turn into the top return path; take items from the front once fully open. To close, pull it back along the rails; do not fold or force it; remove the bottom screws before removing the right plate.
- Columns and upper/lower levels are fixed divisions; dividers are only in the storage zone; nothing can go in the top return zone. Choose items by the actual cell openings; do not treat the outer envelope as usable size.
- There are gaps between the slats, with no seal, closing lock or open lock; test-print to check hinge pins, track friction, nut retention and load first.
- Exported in the display pose; when slicing, turn the large face of the shutter slats and the outer face of the right plate toward the bed. Check supports for the cabinet top/shelves and hinge hole overhangs.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
