## Printing and assembly

- For horizontal drawer use: lift the end gate, load the sliders one by one from the right end along the track, then lower the gate; to reorder, pull them out from the end in the same way.
- Square tenons are reduced 0.4 mm overall per side for 1/4, 3/8 or 1/2 inch square drives; no detent balls, clamping or anti-inversion lock; do not carry the whole tray by the sockets.
- Set spacing by the largest socket outer diameter; the square hole's effective depth must exceed the tenon height. Print the base tray and slider flat base down, and the end gates lying flat; the track bevel is 45°; test-print the actual fit and bridging first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
