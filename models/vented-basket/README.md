## Printing and assembly

- Print base down; check bridging at the top of the vent windows and handle cut-outs in the slicer.
- The handles on both sides are cut through the wall, keeping a closed floor and rim; small items may fall out through the vent windows.
- Dividers are fixed, with their tops 0.4 mm below the lower edge of the handle; with both rows and columns at 1 there are no cells.
- Check the real load-bearing and print quality before use.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
