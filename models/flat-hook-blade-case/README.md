## Intended use

- One spare blade per slot; measure the maximum rectangular outline, not brand. Hooks face left, shanks right; use tweezers or grip the shank, never the tip.
- The loose lid has no opening lock; carry inside a toolbox that restrains it. No puncture, drop or child-safety certification; check fit, grip and blade wear on the print.

## Printing and assembly

- Each slot holds only one spare curved blade lying flat: maximum overall length 50 mm, straight shank 22 mm, sideways bend envelope 12 mm, shank and blade 3.2 mm thick; another 0.6 mm per side; this does not imply fit for a specific brand.
- Hook zone to the left, straight shaft tail to the right. A tail pocket lowers the floor: lift the lid first, then pick up with tweezers or from both sides of the straight shaft; do not touch the blade tip directly.
- Each slot keeps a solid wall, and the base is raised 3 mm to leave room for the handle pocket in the tail section; measure the hook's maximum rectangular envelope, the straight shaft diameter and the actual shaped blade.
- The loose-fit lift-off lid has no anti-opening lock; when carrying, put it in an external toolbox that holds the lid down; not puncture-proof, drop-proof or child-safety certified.
- Print the base box flat on its base; flip the lid when slicing so its flat top is on the bed. Before use, test-print to check fit tolerances, grip feel and wear of the printed surface by metal points.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
