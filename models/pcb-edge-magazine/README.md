# Circuit Board Edge Slot Box

Lower unpowered circuit boards into facing edge slots with room between components. Adjust slot count, board width, edge thickness and component clearance.

## Intended use

- For unpowered boards only, with at least 3 mm of component-free edge. Parameters are clear dimensions; do not squeeze components. Ordinary print material is not antistatic material.

## Printing and assembly

- One-piece, printed flat on its base; bare boards slide down along the vertical slots on both sides without pressing components.
- At least 3 mm of component-free board edge; parameters are actual clearances; ordinary printing material is not anti-static; store only unpowered boards.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
