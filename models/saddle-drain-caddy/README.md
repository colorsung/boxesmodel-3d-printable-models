## Printing and assembly

- Set by the measured width of the sink's middle divider, with 0.6 mm on each side; only suits straight dividers with flat tops and vertical sides; curved or tapered dividers need a separate fit.
- Fit from above so the bridge plate's underside rests on the divider top; the basket compartments on both sides drain straight into the sink. No clamping latch; hold the box when taking sponges, and lift the whole piece straight up to move it.
- Each side's compartments stay open at the top; check sponges or brushes against the generated clear compartment sizes; different size configurations are not one adjustable product.
- You can slice it on its side to check the overhangs of the bridge plate and slot rows; support-free printing is not promised. Wet slipperiness, bridge load, cleaning, drainage and real print fit are still to be tested.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
