## Intended use

- Screws, washers and model parts; rear channels are not storage. No inversion lock; hold the cabinet and support the drawer. Remaining engagement does not establish weight support or tipping resistance.
- Support before pulling the pin; leave at least 18 mm above and one cabinet width ahead. Remove the drawer fully for rear access, refilling or cleaning. Check fit, pin retention and fatigue with a test print.

## Printing and assembly

- Complete set of 3 printed parts: 1 cabinet frame, 1 one-piece widened-front divided drawer and 1 headed stop pin; no purchased parts needed. The front panel and drawer print as one piece; no screws or glue needed.
- The closed front leaves a 0.5 mm nominal sliding gap on each side and 0.8 mm at the top, supported directly at the bottom; front panel width 153.0 mm. An 8 mm side channel is kept at the back for the rear tab and stop pin to move; the channel is not counted as storage space. Confirm printer and filament tolerances with a fit sample.
- First push the drawer all the way in from the front, then insert the stop pin straight down from the cabinet top; the normal forward pull travel is 79.6 mm; it stops when the outer rear lug meets the pin. 0.4 mm single-side clearance in the pin holes; the pin head rests on the cabinet top, with no anti-inversion lock.
- Before fully removing a drawer, support it and hold the cabinet, then pull the pin up; leave at least 18 mm above to pull the pin, and at least one cabinet width in front to take the drawer out fully. Pin total length 16.0 mm, shaft diameter 4 mm, head diameter 10 mm.
- At the stop, 77.0 mm of the drawer bottom is still engaged; this is only geometric support, not a load or tip resistance rating. Hold the cabinet and support the drawer when it is pulled out.
- The closed fill clearance is not the same as finger reach. When partly open, take small items only from the exposed area; to take, refill or clean the back row, pull the pin and remove the whole drawer before working from above.
- Drawer bottom down; slicing the cabinet frame back down with the opening up is recommended; check supports for the top and pin hole bridges. Manufacturing files keep the assembly orientation; rotate them yourself when slicing. Check slicing before printing; before use, check the fit, pin retention, fatigue and load-bearing.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
