## Printing and assembly

- The central shallow tray and four arms are separate, suited to grouping rings, earrings and small accessories; not a jewelry holding fixture.
- The cutaway corners outside the cross run through to the base; length and width are the maximum outer envelope; print base down.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
