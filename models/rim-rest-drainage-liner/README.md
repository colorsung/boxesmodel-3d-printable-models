# Flanged Tapered Drainage Liner

A wide flange rests on an existing pot rim. The tapered liner lifts out as one piece, with nine base holes draining excess water into the outer pot.

## Printing and assembly

- One complete liner, with no custom shafts or fasteners. Supply your existing outer pot. The flange rests on its rim from above.
- Every outer-pot cross section below the rim must clear the corresponding liner diameter with extra tolerance. The inner depth must exceed insertion depth by at least 10 mm.
- For straight or gently tapered outer pots. Rim diameter alone cannot establish fit in a narrow-necked pot; measure the base and narrowest section first.
- After watering, lift out the liner and empty the outer pot; this is a drainage liner without a wick or an automatic watering system.
- Slice with the base down. The flange underside may require supports; inspect the seating surface after removal.
- Preview colors are not included in STL/STEP.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
