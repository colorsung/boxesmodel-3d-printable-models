## Intended use

- Supply cartons and capsules, measuring maximum diameter and total axial length. Align capsule axes front to back; not for random filling or arbitrary brand sizes.
- Hold the carton, lift the bottom capsule about 3.2 mm and pull forward. No automatic single-capsule feed claim; test carton deformation, friction, loaded stability and retrieval with a print.

## Printing and assembly

- With the paper box bottom fully opened, insert it from above down to the step; enter the sleeve opening from the paper box outer size, with 0.4 mm per side. Supply your own paper box and capsules.
- Capsules line up neatly in the paper box with their axes front to back; measure maximum outer diameter and total axial length; not for random filling or any brand size.
- Hold the paper box, gently lift the bottom capsule about 3.2 mm, then take it forward; a 3 mm lip stops items sliding straight out; automatic one-by-one dispensing is not promised.
- Base plate down; check supports for the bridge above the pick-up opening when slicing. Test-print to check paper box friction/deformation, odd-shaped capsules, full-box stability and real use first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
