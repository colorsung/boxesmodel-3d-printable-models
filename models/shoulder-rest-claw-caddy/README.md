## Printing and assembly

- 1 complete printed part; no axles, screws or glue needed. Only for light claw clips that have shoulders on both sides when closed; no spring-open clamp holder is required.
- 3 separate claw clip positions, each 76 mm clear width across; recommended maximum clip length 72 mm; the teeth should not protrude more than 55 mm downward.
- Clear distance between the two shoulders 26 mm, each shoulder 8 mm wide; the teeth envelope should be under 24 mm, and the clip body needs at least 4 mm of real support on each side. Measure the whole claw clip first; curved clips or clips without shoulders do not fit.
- The rear usable depth is 91 mm, and the front hair tie compartment is 40 mm clear deep. The front compartment is only for dry small items; the space under the shoulder is not an extra compartment.
- The clip teeth drop vertically into the central slit from the top, and the shoulders catch the clip body; pinch the clip handle that stands above the shoulder and lift it away; neighboring positions stay in place. Needs a grip part at least 20 mm above the shoulder and clearance above.
- Print flat base down; all shoulders are supported by ribs reaching the floor. Before use, check slicing, a test print, the fit of your specific claw clips and tip resistance; no elastic lock; do not carry the box by the clips.
- Default coral orange, with sage green, cream or misty pink options; all colors use the existing preview panel.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
