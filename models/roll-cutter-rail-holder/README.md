## Intended use

- A prototype for thin paper or film rolls; measure the core. The default Ø28 mm shaft needs a 28.8–30 mm bore. Check cutting, guarding, grip and rail stiffness/friction with a print; the guard has no child-protection certification.
- Supply one complete MARTOR No.45 (45.60) blade, 39×18.5×0.30 mm, two DIN912 M3×10 screws and two DIN934 M3 nuts; use an HS2.5 tool.
- Do not substitute snapped blades, other thicknesses or arbitrary strips. Unload the roll and support the blade at the right service area, following maker instructions. Do not scale hardware; check bed size and use matching part parameters.

## Printing and assembly

- 4 complete printed parts: 1 one-piece roll holder/cutting slot/high square rail frame, 1 double-flange reel, 1 blade front clamp slider and 1 rear guard slider. Rails and axles are all in the files; no custom metal rails or long axles needed.
- Required purchase: 1 complete MARTOR No. 45 (45.60) blade, 39 × 18.5 × 0.30 mm; 2 × DIN 912 M3×10 fully threaded socket head screws (head Ø5.5 × 3 mm, HS2.5); 2 × DIN 934 M3 hex nuts (AF5.5 × 2.4 mm). Do not use snapped-off blades, other thicknesses or arbitrary blade strips. Purchased parts are not shown in the preview and are not included in the STL/STEP.
- Roll width 260, outer diameter 50 mm; axle Ø28 mm; the actual roll core inner diameter must be 28.8–30 mm. With the axle through it, the whole roll drops into the U-supports from above, with both flanges outside the supports; lift straight up to change rolls; the core size is not guessed from brand.
- Without a roll fitted, park the blade carrier in the right-hand service zone. Lay the complete blade diagonally at 45° along its long edge into the front clamp's shallow seat, support the blade and slide the front clamp horizontally onto the square rail from the front; close the rear cover from behind. Put both nuts into the rear hex pockets and tighten 2 M3×10 screws from the front with an HS2.5 hex key. Fit the roll only after the blade is clamped and the track slides.
- To change the blade, take off the roll first; in the right-hand service recess support the front clamp and blade, loosen 2 screws and remove the rear guard, then take the front clamp with the blade out horizontally forward; do not slide the blade out sideways through the middle cutting slot. Follow the blade maker's instructions when handling bare blades; the guard is not child-safety certified.
- The film leads from the top of the roll to the front platform and passes under the rail; the platform is 59 mm high; once the rear guard drops by gravity there is a 0.8 mm film entry gap, and the blade tip enters the 2 mm wide slot 2 mm deep. Hold down the film on both sides, slide once from right to left, take the cut piece and return to the right end without cutting. Slider center range −159 to 159 mm; the full blade passes the roll edges at both ends.
- 6 × 10 mm slide rails, 0.35 mm single-side clearance left and right in the slot, 0.7 mm total upward float; 0.3 mm blade edge gap; fixing hardware and blades do not scale with the box size. The screw tail extends 0.9 mm past the rear guard; all printed parts must use the same parameters.
- A prototype for storing thin paper/film rolls and cutting them by hand. Check slicing and test-print before printing; before use, check cutting quality, clamping force, rail stiffness/friction and guarding.
- With the frame base down, the high rail needs supports and a long-span warp check; blade clips/guards can lie flat side down; measure after cleaning the shallow seat. Check the long frame and axle against your bed width; do not scale the hardware interfaces with the whole package.
- Colors: cream frame/copper clamp/sage guard, light gray frame/lake blue axle/coral clamp, light sand frame/misty purple clamp; colors do not change assembly.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
