## Printing and assembly

- Overall tube outer diameter 49 mm, closed total height 62 mm. The narrowest spool core hole should be at least 1 mm larger than the outer diameter; measure the core hole ribs and usable axial length; universal brand fit is not claimed.
- The vent slits are 1.2 mm wide; use whole granules larger than 1.8 mm at their smallest, and sieve out broken granules and powder first. A solid floor holds the material; dust filtering or a specific moisture absorption rate cannot be promised.
- Fill standing upright; keep granules below the bottom of the thread neck; stop tightening once the lid seats on the shoulder. Seen from above, unscrew counterclockwise; it rises one pitch per turn, and lift off the lid once fully free. No hardware.
- This model is only for storing material in the spool core hole, with no inner dividers or hygrometer hole that could block the fill opening or leak
- Slice with the tube bottom down and the lid top on the build plate; the 0.35 mm nominal thread clearance should be checked with a test print first. Take the desiccant out and regenerate it per its instructions; do not heat the printed tube.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
