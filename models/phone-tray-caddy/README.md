## Intended use

- Measure the phone with its case and allow clearance. The angle is set during generation, not by a moving hinge. Charger not included.
- Set the phone securely before operating it; check tipping resistance, joint strength and weight support with a test print.

## Printing and assembly

- The rest angle is set at generation, measured from vertical; the fixed brace is not a moving hinge. Phone width and thickness must include the case, with your own allowance.
- The rest inserts vertically into the blind slot, with the feet on the deck; a 12 mm cable channel sits in the middle of the front edge; charger not included.
- Steady the phone before using it; test-print to check actual tip resistance, joint strength and load-bearing first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
