## Printing and assembly

- Fold the bag mouth over the outside of the bin, then press the ring down over it; its own weight presses the bag rim; not a seal or strong clamp.
- The ring clearance is a single-side radial size; adjust it to the folded bag material thickness; test-print to check the actual clamping and fit first.
- Remove the knob lid, then drop in small items; lift the whole ring up and out to change the bag. The round bucket has a closed bottom; no liquid seal is claimed.
- Bucket bottom down; flip the ring so its flat shoulder sits on the bed; lid locating face down; check the overhangs when slicing.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
