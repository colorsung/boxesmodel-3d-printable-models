## Printing and assembly

- First fix the hanging strip with 2 of your own 90° countersunk screws (head no larger than the hole + 4 mm and not protruding), then drop the box onto the slope from above.
- The box must be lifted at least 23 mm before it can be taken forward; leave room above to take it out; the base feet rest against the wall.
- Set hole spacing to your actual mounting surface; fits only this set's hanging strip; no external system compatibility or load is promised; check fixing strength after a test print.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
