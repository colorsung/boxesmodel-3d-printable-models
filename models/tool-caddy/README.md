## Printing and assembly

- Box and inner tray base down; flip the box lid 180° so the large flat face is down.
- The inner tray sits on the support ledge; lift the lid first, then the tray, both straight up.
- Fixed 0.4 mm single-side fit clearance; pairs only within this set; no hinges, latches or seals.
- The outer ribs are for styling and thickening; they offer no drop, load or waterproof protection.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
