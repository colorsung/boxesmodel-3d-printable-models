# Parallel Linkage Tiered Box

Parallel links swing the upper level out horizontally, and the upper and lower levels have separate dividers; comes with an open compartment support stand; also needs 8 sets of M3 hardware.

## Printing and assembly

- Rows and columns are set separately for the upper and lower levels; four equal links keep the upper level horizontal, and it fully clears the base compartment when swung to the left.
- Also needs 8 sets of M3 bolts, nuts and washers; axle hole 3.4 mm. The clamping thickness is wall thickness + 6.5 mm; add the actual washer and nut thickness to choose the bolt length.
- First fit the outer four-bar linkage in the empty box, matching the front and rear hole axles; leave the nut room to turn and secure it against loosening. Room for handling the nuts is left beside the holes.
- Hold the base with one hand, move the upper level to the stop, then place the separate support under the upper level; support the upper level and remove the stand before closing.
- The closed display places the separate stand beside the box, and the envelope includes the stand; the open/closed state only changes part placement, not part structure.
- Box bottom and stand bottom down; print the links with their large flat face on the plate, and flip the lid so its top is on the plate. Before use, check real friction, hardware loosening, full-load stability or load-bearing; do not carry the whole box by the upper level.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
