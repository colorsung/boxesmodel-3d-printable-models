## Printing and assembly

- Set by the actual diameter, thickness and count of rigid round coasters; each stands in its own curved-bottom slot and lifts out.
- Thickness clearance per slot is coaster thickness plus 0.8 mm; a larger guard ratio gives higher side support while keeping room at the top to take items.
- The box envelope excludes the coasters; when full, items reach up to about 97.4 mm. Placement stability needs measuring with the coasters' real weight.
- Print flat base down; the base plate stays closed with no drain holes; not a drying rack for wet coasters.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
