## Printing and assembly

- The corner feet go into the next basket's top seats and rest on their ends; this locates them but does not latch; lift 4 mm to separate the upper basket, then take items from the top.
- Solid bottom, with vent windows on all four sides aligned to the compartments; items smaller than the window holes need an inner bag; not a leak-proof container for small granules.
- Each basket has the same layout; stack only with same-size boxes from this set; do not mix with front-access stacking boxes or other crates; test-print to check load-bearing and stability first.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
