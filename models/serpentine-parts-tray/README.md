## Printing and assembly

- Small items are laid out along the switchback channel in work order, with alternating openings for moving material and cleaning; the channels connect, so they cannot replace separate anti-mixing compartments.
- One-piece, printed flat on its base; a manual sorting tray; no automatic feeding mechanism, and pouring does not guarantee materials move in order.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
