## Printing and assembly

- Suits pleated flat-bottom cup filters; enter the base diameter, top diameter and paper height from the real maximum envelope; not a brand size fit chart.
- The stack rise is how much the total height of nested sheets exceeds one sheet's height; it does not indicate the number of sheets. Round bottom notches on both sides help you pinch items from the top.
- 0.8 mm radial clearance; printed outer diameter 114.4 mm, height 51.4 mm, including the feet; 8 mm of the paper edge shows.
- One-piece with no hardware, printed base down; maximum outward slope of the conical side wall is 1. Check slicing before printing; before use, check actual tissue pulling friction, stability and the suitability of food contact materials.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
