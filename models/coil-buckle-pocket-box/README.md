## Printing and assembly

- 1 complete printed part; no hardware, axles or glue to buy. For everyday belts that roll naturally; do not force stiff belts or sharp buckles to bend.
- 2 rolled strap positions, round cavity clear diameter 78 mm; recommended rolled outer diameter up to 76 mm and strap width up to 49 mm; measure including edge paint and decoration thickness; do not squeeze items in.
- Each front buckle compartment is 105.5 mm clear wide and 36 mm clear deep; recommended buckle envelope up to 101.5 × 32 mm. The tail strap passes through a 36 mm wide throat; a tail strap under 34 mm wide is recommended.
- First lay the rolled strap flat into the round compartment from above, then put the attached buckle into the front compartment with the connecting section in the open throat; lift both out together from above; the neighboring compartments do not need emptying.
- The round compartment is open at the front, giving tidy containment rather than locking; keep the box horizontal only and do not transport it upside down; space outside the round compartment does not add capacity.
- Print flat base down; no separate connectors. Check slicing and test-print before printing; before use, check the fit with your specific belts and buckles and long-term deformation.
- Default sage green, with dark blue-gray, cream or warm clay options; preview colors do not change sizes.
- Import at millimeter scale. Check your build volume, orientation, overhangs, supports and mating clearances in the slicer.
